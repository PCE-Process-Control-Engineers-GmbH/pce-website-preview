PCE Expands Its Expertise into Integrated Building Services Engineering

By integrating HVAC and sanitary engineering into its portfolio, PCE is expanding its capabilities in technical building services and multidisciplinary engineering.

Process Control Engineers GmbH is expanding its engineering portfolio to include technical building services. With the addition of heating, ventilation, air-conditioning and sanitary engineering, PCE is strengthening its ability to provide coordinated planning for technical systems and buildings.

Combining HVAC and sanitary engineering with electrical engineering, automation and other engineering disciplines enables technical interfaces to be addressed earlier and more comprehensively. The objective is an integrated building services planning approach in which individual disciplines are coordinated from the beginning, creating consistent designs, clearly defined responsibilities and a reliable technical foundation for demanding industrial and infrastructure projects.
